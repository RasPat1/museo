# Museo — Product Spec

## Vision

Museo helps museum-goers discover, curate, and schedule exhibit visits across cities — starting with Los Angeles.

---

## Core Problem

Keeping up with rotating museum exhibits is tedious. There's no single place to browse current and upcoming exhibits across multiple museums, decide which ones are worth visiting, and get them onto your calendar.

---

## User Stories

### 1. Select Museums
> *As a user, I want to pick the museums I care about (by proximity, personal interest, etc.) so I only see relevant exhibits.*

- Browse museums by city (starting with LA, expanding later)
- Filter by distance, category, or favourites
- Save a personal museum list

### 2. Browse & Evaluate Exhibits
> *As a user, I want to read about each exhibit and decide whether I'm interested — without my calendar becoming cluttered.*

- View current and upcoming exhibits for selected museums
- Read exhibit descriptions, dates, and media
- Mark exhibits as **interested** or **skip**
- Filter by exhibit type or date range

### 3. Generate a Shareable Google Calendar
> *As a user, I want to click a button and get a Google Calendar populated only with the exhibits I chose, that I can share with friends.*

- One-click calendar generation from selected exhibits
- Creates a **dedicated** Google Calendar (not mixed into personal events)
- Calendar is shareable via link

---

## Future Ideas

- **Monthly digest**: On the 1st of each month, receive an email summarising newly announced exhibits across your selected museums.
- **Surprise alerts**: Get notified about pop-up or limited-run exhibits that appear without much advance notice.
- **Multi-city expansion**: Extend beyond LA to other cities.
