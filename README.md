# Museo

**Discover, curate, and calendar museum exhibits — effortlessly.**

Museo is a tool that lets you browse current and upcoming museum exhibits, pick the ones you actually want to see, and generate a shareable Google Calendar with a single click.

## How It Works

1. **Choose your museums** — Select the museums you care about, filtered by city or personal interest.
2. **Browse exhibits** — Read about each exhibit and mark the ones that catch your eye.
3. **Generate a calendar** — One click creates a dedicated, shareable Google Calendar with your picks.
